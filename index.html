07. Profile & Background

Curiosity Rooted in How Things Hold Together

"An object is not just a form; it is a physical conversation between materials, human hands, and everyday rituals."

I am Thanush Puvanesvaran, an industrial designer currently undertaking my degree in Industrial Design at Universiti Sultan Zainal Abidin (UniSZA) in Terengganu, Malaysia.

My approach connects industrial discipline with craft sensitivity. Whether I am carving miniature timber hulls for cultural souvenirs or generating parametric CAD housings for audio hardware and ultrasonic appliances, I focus on the moment when an abstract concept gains weight, texture, and a genuine reason to exist.
